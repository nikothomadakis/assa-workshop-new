1. Executive assessment

Protection liabilities increased from R97,449,936 to R105,260,319, a net movement of +R7,810,383. New business and claims variance are the two material components under the initial investigation procedure. Lapse, expense and net basis-change amounts fall below its thresholds.

The bridge includes a +R430,261 residual, within the procedure’s tolerance. These initial findings have not undergone HAF review.

2. Basis of investigation

Procedure LM-2024.2 applies materiality when an absolute component exceeds 25% of absolute net movement OR 2% of opening liability. The thresholds are R1,952,595.75 and R1,948,998.72; exceeding either is sufficient. Expected change is treated as a baseline.

Experience investigation covers the two largest cohort contributions. Basis changes are assessed at net level. Residual tolerance is 0.5% of opening liability (R487,249.68); a within-tolerance residual is disclosed without further investigation. Source signs are retained.

Net basis change is +R168,880, below both initial thresholds. The assumption register and individual impacts were not retrieved. Under this procedure, the net result does not trigger investigation of offsetting underlying changes.

6. New business and interpretation

New business contributes +R9,509,035 on a recognition-date basis. The 97 Q2 entrants have a separate closing liability of R8,654,124; this balance is not another bridge component. Of these policies, 53 (54.6%) are aged 60+, holding R7,132,241 (82.4% of the entrants’ closing liability).

The product note attributes growth to the April–June Silver Cover campaign targeting ages 55+. The measured mix is consistent with that commentary, but no campaign identifier or attribution study establishes causation.

7. Reporting position and limitations

The initial procedure identifies new business and claims as the principal movements requiring explanation. The within-tolerance residual remains unexplained. Coverage is limited to the selected claims cohorts, net assumption effects and new-business policy analysis. The closing balance has not been independently supported by whole-book policy totals.

Evidence used: Protection movement extract; claims experience for the two older cohorts; Q2-entrant policy aggregates; product note; initial procedure LM-2024.2. These findings await review under the current reporting requirements.
