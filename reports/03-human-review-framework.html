Human review framework

Framework underlying the human reference report

Period: 2025Q2 · 31 March–30 June 2025   |   Currency: ZAR, whole rand

Scope: Protection product only   |   Status: Current requirements for the exercise

The human reference uses the following basis to judge whether the investigation gives a sufficiently supported account of the quarter.

1. Scope and authority

The review covers Protection, 2025Q2, in ZAR. Dates and populations must be comparable. Current requirements take precedence over older procedures. For closing liability, I rely on policy records for the same scope and date; any correction to a conflicting summary needs evidence and disclosure.

2. Materiality

Amounts greater than 10% of absolute net movement OR 1% of opening liability, assessed in absolute terms, require investigation. Net movement is closing less opening. Either threshold suffices; use validated balances and recalculate after corrections. If net movement is zero, use the opening threshold.

Material supporting contributions deserve attention regardless of their ranking. Individual assumption changes should be considered before netting. Expected change remains a baseline; new business and its mix always need explanation.

3. Evidence and reconciliation

A balanced report is not necessarily a reliable one. Balances need support from underlying records; subtotals, signs, units and relevant calculations must agree. Consider missing or duplicate evidence and avoid mixing balances, movements and counts.

An unexplained residual may be accepted within 0.5% of opening liability, with disclosure. That tolerance cannot justify a known error or conflicting source balances. Management commentary may help interpret results, but should not be presented as proven causation. Request missing evidence through the investigator.

4. Acceptance

Focus revision requests on matters that affect confidence in the findings, and raise them together. Approve once discrepancies and errors are resolved, material conclusions are supported and remaining uncertainty is clear. Optional analysis or stylistic improvements should not delay an otherwise sound report.
