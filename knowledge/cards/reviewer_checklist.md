# Reviewer checklist

The workshop review step is the HAF agent. Attendees develop its review framework in the HAF System Message. This card describes the review interface; it is not a completed HAF prompt or an acceptance standard.

## Evidence available to the reviewer

The HAF receives the original question and the written findings, method, source references, figures, calculations and limitations. It does not receive hidden execution history or direct access to the six source-data tools. Its calculator can check supplied arithmetic. Request further retrieval through the investigator when evidence is insufficient.

## Building the review framework

Define the role and authority of the reviewer, the applicable basis of review, required checks, the evidence needed to complete those checks, and the conditions for acceptance or revision. Make instructions general enough to apply to other investigations. Identify which rules govern when an initial investigation procedure differs from the current review requirements.

The separate workshop guidance provides prompt-building headings and examples of how to write useful checks. The workflow leaves the HAF System Message blank for attendees to complete.

## Returning a decision

Follow the supplied response format. State the review requirements actually applied, preserve the complete current findings and supporting evidence, and give specific correction requests where needed. Do not claim fresh source-tool calls or accept unsupported numerical corrections as facts.

On revision, the investigator follows the current HAF requirements and retrieves any needed evidence through the same tools. The HAF reviews the complete revised findings before accepting them. Approval is not automatic when the revision limit is reached.
